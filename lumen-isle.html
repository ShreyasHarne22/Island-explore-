Island Explore

by Shreyas Harne

An open ocean world of one hundred islands. Clear an island of every light and beast, then swim to the golden gate to sail on. Pick any cleared island below to revisit it.

WASD or the joystick to move, Space or Jump to jump, drag to turn the camera. The ocean never ends, so you can swim anywhere. Press R to return to the last safe beach, F to call and pet your companion (finish levels to earn coins for new pets), and E or a click to shoot. Giant crabs, boars and sharks hunt here, and huge ogres wait inside the caves. Clear islands to upgrade your gun (Q or 1 to 4 switches weapons): you have six hearts, and they slowly come back.
